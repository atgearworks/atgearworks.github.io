ZIP ONE Support

Support page for ZIP ONE provided by AT GEAR WORKS.

About the App

App Name: ZIP ONE

Category: Utilities

Overview: ZIP ONE is a utility app that allows you to easily and securely create ZIP archives on your iPhone. It supports AES‑256 encrypted ZIPs for high‑security use, suitable for both business and personal needs. The app includes photo‑to‑ZIP conversion (with automatic HEIC→JPG conversion), automatic removal of EXIF data, strong password generation, password history management, and iCloud Keychain syncing for secure multi‑device use. The Pro version unlocks unlimited password history access and biometric protection, offering an enhanced and secure ZIP creation environment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Can I create password‑protected ZIP files?
A. Yes, ZIP ONE supports creating AES‑256 encrypted ZIP files.

Q. Why does ZIP ONE use AES‑256 instead of ZipCrypto?
A. ZipCrypto is outdated and can be cracked within seconds. AES‑256 is a modern, highly secure encryption method used by banks and government agencies.

Q. Windows cannot open my ZIP file. What should I do?
A. Windows Explorer does not support AES‑256 ZIP files. Please use extraction tools such as “7‑Zip”.

Q. Where are ZIP files saved?
A. After creating a ZIP file, choose “Save to Files” from the share sheet to save it to iCloud Drive or any folder on your device. ZIP ONE does not auto‑save files.

Q. Are there limits on file count or size when creating ZIPs?
A. Large files or many photos may fail due to iOS memory limits. If this happens, try splitting the files into smaller ZIPs.

Q. Can ZIP ONE convert HEIC photos?
A. Yes. HEIC photos are automatically converted to JPG before ZIP compression.

Q. Can ZIP ONE remove EXIF data?
A. Yes. Location, capture time, and other personal metadata can be automatically removed.

Q. Can ZIP ONE generate passwords automatically?
A. Yes. You can generate strong passwords with a single tap.

Q. Where is password history stored?
A. Password history is stored on your device and synced securely via iCloud Keychain.

Q. What is the difference between the free and Pro versions?
A. Free users can view their latest three password entries. Pro users can access unlimited history, including entries created in the free version.

Q. Are repeated passwords saved to history?
A. Yes. In the latest version, repeated passwords are saved correctly.

Q. What is biometric protection used for?
A. It protects access to your password history. Face ID or Touch ID is required to unlock it.

Q. Is biometric protection available in the free version?
A. No. Biometric protection is a Pro‑only feature.

Q. Biometric authentication is not working. What should I do?
A. Please ensure Face ID or Touch ID is enabled in your device settings.

Q. How many ZIP creations are available per day in the free version?
A. Free users have a daily usage limit. Watching a rewarded ad grants additional usage.

Q. I watched a rewarded ad but my usage did not increase.
A. The latest version fixes an issue where watching an ad incorrectly consumed two daily uses.

Q. Does password history sync across devices?
A. Yes. History is synced via iCloud Keychain.

Q. What happens if iCloud Keychain is disabled?
A. Password history is stored only on the device and will not sync to other devices.

Q. The app is not working properly. What should I do?
A. Please restart the app or your device. If the issue persists, contact support.
